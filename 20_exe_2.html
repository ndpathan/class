Welcome to Bhavnagar City

1. Overview

Bhavnagar is a major city located in the Saurashtra region of Gujarat, India. It was founded in 1723 AD by Bhavsinhji Gohil and served as the capital of the Bhavnagar Princely State.

2. Culture and Tradition

The city is widely famous for its rich cultural heritage, educational institutions, and delicious Bhavnagari Gathiya. The people of Bhavnagar are known for their warm hospitality and peaceful nature.

3. Major Attractions

Some popular tourist places in and around Bhavnagar include:
• Takhteshwar Temple - Located on a hill offering a panoramic city view.
• Victoria Park - A famous nature reserve inside the city.
• Velavadar Blackbuck National Park - Known for blackbucks and bird watching.
